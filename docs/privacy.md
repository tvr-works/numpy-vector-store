# NumPy Vector Store privacy

<p class="privacy-notice__date">Last updated <time datetime="2026-11-24">November 24, 2026</time></p>

TVR Works LLC operates vectors.tvr.works. This notice covers visits to that website and use of the NumPy Vector Store Python library.

## Website visits

Cloudflare helps deliver and protect this site. It processes request information such as your IP address and the pages you request. Your browser may [send Cloudflare a report](https://developers.cloudflare.com/network-error-logging/) if it has trouble connecting to the site. You can read the documentation without signing in. See [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Your browser stores your choice of light, dark, or system appearance. Your browser searches a downloaded index as you type. When you open a result, the page request includes your search terms so the page can highlight them.

We do not add advertising or visitor analytics scripts to these pages.

## Data in your application

NumPy Vector Store runs inside your Python application. Your application decides which vectors and metadata to pass to it and where to save any archives. If you use an embedding provider or host your application with another service, you choose which data to send to that service.

## Support and privacy questions

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments. We use what you send to respond and follow up. Other people can read what you post in a public GitHub issue.

For privacy questions, or to ask about or request deletion of information you sent us, email [tim@tvr.works](mailto:tim@tvr.works).
